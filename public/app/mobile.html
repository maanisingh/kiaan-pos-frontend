📡 NFC Card Activation Guide

Turn your phone into an NFC reader and manage your card digitally

1

Enable NFC

Go to Settings → Connections → Enable NFC on your phone

2

Open App

Launch Kiaan Wallet and tap "Scan NFC Card"

3

Hold Card

Place your NFC card on the back of your phone

4

Confirm PIN

Enter your 4-digit card PIN to activate

✨ Once activated, use your phone for NFC payments at any Kiaan POS terminal!
